# NeuroScan AI — Clinical & Regulatory Disclaimer

---

## 1. Non-Diagnostic Medical Device Status

**NeuroScan AI is an artificial intelligence research platform and informational clinical decision-support prototype.** 

- It is **NOT** cleared, approved, or CE-marked by the United States Food and Drug Administration (FDA), European Medicines Agency (EMA), or any other regulatory authority as a medical diagnostic software or software-as-a-medical-device (SaMD).
- The predictions, probability distributions, risk tiers, and clinical AI explanations produced by this platform do **NOT** constitute a medical diagnosis, definitive prognosis, or clinical treatment recommendation.
- This platform must never be used as a standalone or primary method for diagnosing Alzheimer's Disease, Mild Cognitive Impairment (MCI), vascular dementia, or any other neurodegenerative disease.

---

## 2. Mandatory Physician & Radiologist Review

- All image classifications, volumetric assessments, and PDF reports generated by NeuroScan AI must be thoroughly reviewed and interpreted by a certified neurologist, neuroradiologist, or licensed healthcare physician.
- Clinical diagnoses of Alzheimer's Disease require multimodal clinical evaluations, including:
  - Standardized cognitive assessments: Mini-Mental State Examination (MMSE), Montreal Cognitive Assessment (MoCA), Clinical Dementia Rating (CDR).
  - Patient neurological and psychiatric history.
  - Biomarker assays: Cerebrospinal Fluid (CSF) tau/amyloid-beta ratios, amyloid PET imaging.
  - Blood-based plasma p-tau217 or p-tau181 testing.

---

## 3. Patient Confidentiality, HIPAA, and GDPR

1. **De-identification:** All MRI scans uploaded to NeuroScan AI should be stripped of all Protected Health Information (PHI) and direct identifiers (patient names, medical record numbers, dates of birth) in compliance with the HIPAA Safe Harbor method and EU GDPR pseudonymization mandates prior to processing.
2. **Ephemeral File Processing:** Uploaded MRI images are stored temporarily for inference in sanitized workspace directories and immediately cleared via secure removal routines (`safe_remove`).
3. **Audit Trails:** All diagnostic access events are logged into structured rotating audit logs (`logs/clinical_audit.log`) without storing raw patient credentials or unencrypted biometrics.

---

## 4. Algorithmic Limitations & Potential Bias

- **Training Distribution:** The ResNet-18 model was trained on curated research datasets (such as OASIS / ADNI). Real-world hospital scanners with varied field strengths (1.5T vs. 3.0T), non-standard slice thicknesses, motion artifacts, or severe metal implants may yield degraded or inconclusive predictions.
- **Low-Confidence Safeguards:** Whenever model confidence falls below the validated threshold (`PREDICTION_THRESHOLD`, default `0.30`), the platform deliberately suppresses categorical diagnoses and assigns an `Uncertain / Inconclusive` classification with explicit guidance to seek clinical consultation.
